## Project Summary

This project aims to build a foundational e-commerce storefront for an online pharmacy. The scope includes a main dashboard displaying over-the-counter products in a grid, a product detail page with descriptions and an 'Add to Cart' button, and basic shopping cart functionality. The user interface will be responsive, accessible, and designed for a simple, intuitive user experience. Checkout and payment processing are explicitly out of scope for this phase.

## Business Context

The development will be informed by industry best practices for online pharmacies. Key insights from the provided research include the dominance of the Over-the-Counter (OTC) market segment, the critical importance of a mobile-first experience, and the necessity of building user trust. The design will incorporate standard UX patterns like a grid-based layout and clear calls-to-action. Non-functional requirements are heavily influenced by the research, emphasizing page load speeds under 3 seconds, high availability (99.9%), and strict adherence to accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA), which is a regulatory requirement for healthcare organizations. Security practices like SSL/TLS encryption will be implemented from the start, even though payment processing is out of scope, to establish a secure foundation.

## User Stories

### US-001 — View Product Catalog

**As a** shopper
**I want** to see a visual grid of all available over-the-counter medications and health supplies on the main page
**So that** I can browse the products offered by the pharmacy.

- **Epic:** EPIC-01: Product Discovery
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 3

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on the storefront's main page, When the page loads, Then I see a grid of product images.
- Given the product grid is displayed, When I view a product, Then I can see its name and price displayed clearly with the image.
- Given the catalog is loading, When I visit the page, Then a loading indicator is displayed until the products appear.

### US-002 — View Product Details

**As a** shopper
**I want** to click on a product image in the catalog
**So that** I can view a detailed page with more information about that specific product.

- **Epic:** EPIC-01: Product Discovery
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 3
- **Depends on:** US-001
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am viewing the product catalog, When I click a product image, Then the view transitions to a dedicated product detail page.
- Given I am on the product detail page, Then I can see an enlarged image, the product name, price, and a full description.
- Given I am viewing the product description, Then it contains sections for 'Active Ingredients' and 'Basic Dosage Instructions'.
- Given I am on the product detail page, Then an 'Add to Cart' button is clearly visible and enabled.

### US-004 — Add Product to Cart

**As a** shopper
**I want** to click the 'Add to Cart' button on a product detail page
**So that** I can add the item to my shopping cart for a future purchase.

- **Epic:** EPIC-02: Shopping Cart Management
- **Priority:** critical
- **Story Points:** 5
- **Depends on:** US-002
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I am on a product detail page, When I click the 'Add to Cart' button, Then I receive a confirmation that the item has been added (e.g., a toast notification or a change in the cart icon).
- Given an item has been added to the cart, When I add the same item again, Then the quantity of that item in the cart increases by one.
- Given the cart is not visible, When I add an item, Then a cart indicator (e.g., an icon with a number) updates to show the total number of items.

### US-005 — View Shopping Cart Contents

**As a** shopper
**I want** to view the items I have added to my cart
**So that** I can review my selections before proceeding.

- **Epic:** EPIC-02: Shopping Cart Management
- **Priority:** high
- **Story Points:** 3
- **Depends on:** US-004
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given I have added items to my cart, When I click on the cart icon, Then a summary of my cart is displayed (e.g., in a modal or on a separate page).
- Given I am viewing the cart summary, Then for each item I can see its name, image, price, and quantity.
- Given I am viewing the cart summary, Then the total price for all items is calculated and displayed.


## Non-Functional Requirements

- **performance** (high): The product catalog and product detail pages must achieve a Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) of less than 3 seconds on a standard broadband connection.- **security** (critical): All communication between the user's browser and the server must be encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.- **accessibility** (critical): The entire user interface must comply with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.- **usability** (high): The application layout must be fully responsive and provide an optimal viewing experience on common desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.- **reliability** (medium): The storefront application must maintain 99.9% uptime to ensure continuous access for customers.- **security** (high): The application must not store any sensitive user data in cookies or local storage without appropriate security measures.
## Assumptions

- Product data, including names, descriptions, pricing, active ingredients, dosage, and high-quality images, will be provided via a separate mechanism (e.g., a CMS or API).
- Since user authentication is out of scope, the shopping cart state will be managed on the client-side (e.g., using browser local storage).
- The visual design, branding guidelines, and logo will be provided separately.
- The application will be built for a single language (English) and currency (USD).
